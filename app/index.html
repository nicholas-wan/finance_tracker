How the numbers and checks work
  • Card Payment and Rebates rows are excluded from spending; refunds reduce totals. A refund nets against the same merchant's charges on its own day first, then up to 7 earlier days.
  • 6M/12M figures are means over prior complete statement months; the current partial month is excluded.
  • Suspicious checks: unreversed same-day duplicates (≥ S$40 total); a first-seen merchant day ≥ S$1,500; a charge ≥ 4× anything in a short history (and ≥ S$500); ≥ 10× the merchant's median once 10 rows exist (and ≥ S$300); 5+ same-day charges ≥ S$300; a stable subscription jumping in price; a first foreign-currency charge ≥ S$200 (Games and Travel exempt); a credit ≥ S$200 from a merchant that never charged this card.
  • Insurance and Fees & charges run only the duplicate and burst checks — premiums are large by nature.
  • Checks read the merchant-rule category, so re-labelling a transaction never silences its signal. Recognition is per signal: a new kind of alert on the same rows re-surfaces.

Review prompts use statement history only and are not fraud verdicts.

  • Transfers, investments, SRS, fixed deposits, and card-bill payments are movements of money, not bank-account spending.
  • Flagged for review: unclassified flows; outgoing transfers ≥ S$500; other withdrawals ≥ S$1,000; a first sizeable payment (≥ S$500) to a new counterparty; identical same-day outgoing movements ≥ S$40 total; amounts derived from the running balance; unreconciled sources.
  • Money in never needs review. Outgoing movements under S$50 stay quiet too — everyday food-sized spending — unless they repeat on the same day, since a possible double charge is worth a look at any size.
  • Trusted counterparties skip the amount and new-counterparty checks (data-quality checks still apply) and match by exact name, except that an entry ending in * also matches the longer forms of a name the statement truncates. Currently trusted: .
  • mBK funds-transfer references are destination account numbers: known accounts show their name, unknown ones show the account number itself.
  • 6M/12M spending averages are means over prior complete statement months and exclude internal movements.
